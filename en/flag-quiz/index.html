Flag quiz

With The Grand Tour you learn the flags of all 197 countries by looking at them properly, not by cramming.

In Name the flag a flag appears and you pick the country from four. Find the flag turns it round: you are given a country and look for its flag. Lookalikes puts flags that are easy to mix up side by side, such as Romania and Chad, so you finally see the difference. In Flag colours one colour has been taken out of the flag and you say which it was.

Countries you get wrong come back more often, and countries you know come back less, so a few minutes a day is enough. Everything runs in your browser, with no account and no ads, and it works on a phone.
