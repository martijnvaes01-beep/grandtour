Grand Tour

Rivers, mountains and seas quiz

Most geography quizzes stop at countries. Grand Tour also asks where the Danube runs, where the Andes stand and which sea is which, on a globe you spin with your finger.

In Trace the river a name appears and you find that river on the globe. Find the range does the same for mountain ranges. In Seas and oceans a stretch of water is lit up and you choose its name from four. If you are stuck, the game shows you where it was.

Countries you get wrong come back more often, and countries you know come back less, so a few minutes a day is enough. Everything runs in your browser, with no account and no ads, and it works on a phone.
