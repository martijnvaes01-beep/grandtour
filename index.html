The Grand Tour, a geography quiz

The Grand Tour is a free geography game for your phone or computer. You spin a globe, name flags, find capitals and trace rivers, and whatever you get wrong comes back until you know it.

Every day there are five new games, the same for everybody: one try, with a clock on every answer. That is how you can compare your score with other players.

  • Flag quiz
  • Capitals quiz
  • Rivers, mountains and seas quiz
